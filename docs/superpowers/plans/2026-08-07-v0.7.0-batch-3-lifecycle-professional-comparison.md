## Public Contract Decisions

Use these names consistently in Python, JSON, and TypeScript:

```text
CaseLifecycleState = active | trashed | purging
DatasetStatus += abandoned
DataPreparationState = needs_upload | needs_mapping | needs_quality_review | ready | blocked
DataPreparationStep = upload | mapping | quality_review | experiment | repair
PurgeOperationState = prepared | quarantined | committed | cleaned | rolled_back | failed
canonical confirmation query = professional_confirmation
comparison selection = 2..4 unique candidate_result_ids
```

`DatasetStatus.blocked` continues to mean a quality-blocked dataset. A valid blocked dataset resolves to `data_preparation.state=needs_mapping` with a “修正字段映射” action. `data_preparation.state=blocked` is reserved for missing/corrupt/out-of-root files, hash mismatch, broken ownership, or an unknown persisted status.
